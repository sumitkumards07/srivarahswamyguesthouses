About Varahaswamy Guest House in Tirumala

Varahaswamy Guest House is private accommodation for visitors staying in Tirumala. The property offers room options for pilgrims, families and groups visiting the temple.

We provide comfortable rooms ranging from non-AC options to spacious family accommodations. Our facilities include clean attached bathrooms, 24/7 water supply, and backup power to ensure a pleasant stay.

Our location on the hilltop offers convenient access to the main temple complex. For booking, we recommend reserving your dates in advance. Please contact us directly to confirm availability and plan your visit.

Seasonal & Booking Tips

During peak seasons, Brahmotsavam, weekends and festival days, rooms in Tirumala fill quickly. We recommend early Tirumala hotel booking through our website. Booking in advance ensures comfortable stay in Tirumala without last-minute stress.
