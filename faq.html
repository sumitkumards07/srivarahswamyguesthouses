Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about your stay in Tirumala.

Where is Varahaswamy Guest House located?

Our guest house is located top on hill in Tirumala, near the temple area, making it convenient for darshan and temple visits.

How can I book a room?

You can book rooms easily through our website using the "Book Now" buttons or by contacting us directly on WhatsApp at +91 7850064448.

Is Varaha Swamy Guest House near Tirumala Temple?

Yes, our guest house is located very near the Tirumala Temple, making it an ideal stay for devotees.

Do you provide family rooms in Tirumala?

Yes, we offer both AC and Non-AC family rooms suitable for 3–5 guests, as well as large group rooms for up to 8 guests.

What are the check-in and check-out timings?

Our standard check-in time is flexible based on your arrival, and check-out is typically 24 hours from the time of check-in, subject to availability.

Is Varaswamy Guest House the same as Varahaswamy Guest House?

Yes, Varaswamy Guest House is just a common alternative spelling for Varahaswamy Guest House used by many devotees.
