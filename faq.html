Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about your stay in Tirumala.

What is Varahaswamy Guest House?

Sri Varahaswamy Guest House is a private accommodation option offering rooms for pilgrims, families and visitors.

Where is Varahaswamy Guest House located?

Our guest house is located top on hill in Tirumala, making it convenient for darshan and temple visits.

How can I book Varahaswamy Guest House?

You can book rooms easily through our website using the "Book Now" buttons or by contacting us directly on WhatsApp.

Is Varahaswamy Guest House in Tirumala?

Yes, it is located directly in Tirumala top on hill.

What room options are available?

We offer both AC and Non-AC family rooms suitable for 3–5 guests, as well as large group rooms for up to 8 guests.

How far is the guest house from Tirumala Temple?

Our guest house is located very near the Tirumala Temple, making it an ideal stay for devotees.
